# ECSA ASISAS 8-Page Plan

## Venue Target

- Venue: ECSA 2026 ASISAS workshop
- Target type: Short & Position Paper
- Length target: 8 pages main body + 2 pages references
- This plan prepares a submission copy without changing implementation or frozen artifact semantics.

## Main Compression Goal

The current full-paper draft is much longer than the venue limit. The goal is to preserve the core contribution while compressing detail into a concise workshop paper.

## Core Message to Preserve

Scanner tells what may go wrong.
AEP records what actually happened.
Validator determines whether the execution is acceptable.
FDO/data-space environments can preserve and reference the resulting evidence object.

## Proposed 8-Page Section Structure

1. Introduction
2. Background and Related Work
3. Risk-linked AEP Model
4. Prototype and Evaluation
5. Discussion and Limitations
6. Conclusion

Some material currently separated into sections 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9 of the full draft will need to be merged into a smaller workshop structure.

## Compression Principle

- keep the contribution and evidence-linkage argument
- keep PASS / WARN / FAIL distinction
- keep the minimal AEP model
- keep one compact prototype description
- keep one compact evaluation section
- keep one explicit limitations section
- move planning and internal preparation details out of the paper body
